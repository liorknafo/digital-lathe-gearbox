One rule for every height: measure from the flat face of the blue panel. A number is + if the part sticks out towards you, and − if it sits behind the panel face (in a recess).
  1. Heights (orange letters on the side views): rest the end of the caliper on the part's face, keep the caliper square to the panel, and push the depth rod down onto the blue panel. Read the number.
  2. Diameters and thickness: use the big jaws.
  3. Take the leadscrew gear and the 21T stud gear off first (you did that for the photos already).
  4. Leave any box empty if you can't reach it. The grey numbers are my estimates from your photos.
